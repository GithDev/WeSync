WeSync keeps folders in sync across your own devices — phone, laptop, desktop —
without any cloud account or central server. Your files travel directly between
your devices over your own network.

Built on Syncthing, WeSync wraps it in a simple, one-knob experience:

* Pair devices you trust, then share a folder — that is the whole setup.
* No cloud, no account, no third party ever holds your files.
* Power-aware on Android: WeSync only wakes and syncs when it makes sense, so
  it stays light on your battery instead of running around the clock.
* Stays quiet on networks you have not chosen — it won't announce itself on a
  café or guest Wi-Fi.

WeSync is free and open source.
